Data Privacy Notice

PUP Motor Pool Management System

In compliance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012, the PUP Motor Pool Management System ensures that all collected personal data shall be processed fairly, securely, and lawfully.

Information collected through this system includes user identity, transportation request details, and related operational records.

Appropriate security measures are implemented to protect personal data from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

Users may contact the university administration regarding concerns related to personal data handling.
