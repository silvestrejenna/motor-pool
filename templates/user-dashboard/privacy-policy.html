Privacy Policy

PUP Motor Pool Management System

The PUP Motor Pool Management System respects and protects the privacy of all users. Personal information collected through this system shall only be used for transportation request processing, trip ticket management, and official university operations.

User information will not be shared with unauthorized individuals or external organizations unless required by university policy or applicable laws.

By using this system, users agree to the collection and processing of necessary information for operational purposes.
