User Guide / Manual

PUP Motor Pool Management System

Step 1: Log In

Use your registered university email and password to access the Motor Pool Management System.

Step 2: Submit Request

Open the New Request page and complete the transportation request form with complete trip details.

Step 3: Wait for Approval

Your request will be reviewed by authorized Motor Pool personnel.

Step 4: View Requests

Monitor your request status through the My Request page.

Step 5: Access Trip Tickets

Approved trip tickets can be downloaded from the Trip Tickets page.
